YOUR LEARNING TOOLKIT

Here is how to explore.

When you enter the app, open Mini Lessons to see the course menu. Each section works like a subject: tap an unlocked title to open its full notes.

▤

Mini lessons

Choose the first available section, read the notes, explore vocabulary, check English translations, and listen to example phrases.

🔐

Complete to unlock

At the end of a section, select “Move to the next section.” Confirm “I testify, I have completed it” to record your completion and unlock the next section.

☆

Dictionary and practice

Search Arabic or English words, save words for revision, listen to pronunciation where available, and try the quick quiz.

Remember: your lesson progress is saved in this browser on this device. If you clear browser data or switch devices, your progress may not carry over.
