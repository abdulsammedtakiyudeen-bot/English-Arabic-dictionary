ADSENSE SETUP — READ BEFORE ACTIVATING

This project contains a disabled manual-ad framework. No ads are requested while ads-config.js has enabled:false or blank IDs.

1. AdSense account ownership and approval
   AdSense account holders must meet Google's age and eligibility rules. As the site owner is under 18, a parent/guardian must own and manage any AdSense account and payment profile where required. Do not enter a false date of birth.
2. Apply using the actual public website and comply with Google's current site, content, privacy and consent requirements.
3. After approval, copy your own publisher ID (format ca-pub-...) and each manual ad-unit ID from your AdSense account.
4. In ads-config.js set enabled:true, publisherId to your publisher ID, and fill the slot IDs. Never use example IDs.
5. Add a slot only to a page and position where it is clearly separated from navigation, buttons, quizzes, exams and download controls. Do not place ads on payment, sign-in, certificate or exam screens.
6. This script checks the signed-in user's Supabase profiles membership and premium_until before requesting ads. If membership cannot be checked, it fails closed and does not load ads. A browser-side check is a display rule, not a security boundary.
7. Premium pages must not contain AdSense slots. Do not enable site-wide Auto ads unless Premium URLs and sensitive pages are excluded in AdSense settings.
8. Google controls whether an ad fills a slot and what creative is shown. A slot does not guarantee an ad will appear or that Google will follow an exact creative layout.
9. Update privacy.html to describe the actual advertising cookies/data processing and add any legally required consent mechanism before enabling ads. Do not claim ads are active before they are.
10. Test Free and Premium accounts, logged-out state, mobile layout and all sensitive pages before launch.
