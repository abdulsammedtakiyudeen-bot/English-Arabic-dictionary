About Islamic Arabic Learning Dictionary

Islamic Arabic Learning Dictionary is a beginner-friendly educational project created to help learners explore Arabic vocabulary, everyday expressions, basic grammar, and commonly used Islamic terms.

Our purpose

We aim to make early Arabic study approachable by presenting Arabic script, transliteration, English meanings, examples, short lessons, and practice activities in one place. Learners are encouraged to study a little at a time and revisit words regularly.

How to use the site

  • Search for Arabic words or English meanings.
  • Read the lesson notes and examples before practising.
  • Use audio or listening controls where available to practise pronunciation.
  • Review saved words and quizzes to reinforce learning.

Accuracy and religious learning

Translations and transliterations are learning aids and may not capture every nuance. Arabic meaning depends on context. This website is not a substitute for a qualified Arabic teacher, reliable Qur'an translation and tafsir, or a knowledgeable scholar for religious rulings.

Who makes it?

This independent learning project is created by Dev. Takiyudeen. Content and features may be improved as the project develops.
