Important implementation note:
The Premium display follows the website's existing client-side membership-check pattern. As the website is hosted as static GitHub Pages content, hiding a section in the browser is not a server-side security boundary. Do not treat client-side-only visibility as protection for highly sensitive paid content. A future server/database-backed content delivery policy would be needed for strong content protection.

Religious-content note:
Translations are concise learning renderings, not reproduced full copyrighted translations. Qur'anic story summaries are paraphrases with references. Hadith references are linked to their collections. Check Arabic and source references with a qualified teacher before expanding this material.
