Greetings and polite expressions

As-salāmu ʿalaykum
Peace be upon you.

Wa ʿalaykumu s-salām
And peace be upon you too.

Shukran jazīlan
Thank you very much.
مِنْ فَضْلِكَ
Min faḍlika
Please (to a male).
FREE · Grammar

Build a simple nominal sentence

A basic Arabic nominal sentence often begins with a noun or pronoun. The first part is the topic (مُبْتَدَأ), and the information about it is the predicate (خَبَر).

الْبَيْتُ كَبِيرٌ
Al-baytu kabīrun
The house is big.
الطَّالِبُ مُجْتَهِدٌ
Aṭ-ṭālibu mujtahidun
The student is hardworking.

Notice that Arabic often does not use a present-tense word for “is” in this sentence pattern.

FREE · Conversation

Introducing yourself

مَا اسْمُكَ؟
Mā ismuka?
What is your name? (to a male)
اسْمِي أَحْمَدُ
Ismī Aḥmadu
My name is Ahmad.
مِنْ أَيْنَ أَنْتَ؟
Min ayna anta?
Where are you from? (to a male)
أَنَا مِنْ غَانَا
Anā min Ghānā
I am from Ghana.
FREE · Reading

Short reading: My school

أَنَا طَالِبٌ. أَدْرُسُ فِي مَدْرَسَةٍ. أُحِبُّ اللُّغَةَ الْعَرَبِيَّةَ.

Transliteration: Anā ṭālibun. Adrusu fī madrasatin. Uḥibbu l-lughata l-ʿarabiyyata.

Meaning: I am a student. I study at a school. I love the Arabic language.

Check your understanding

What language does the student love? The Arabic language (اللُّغَةَ الْعَرَبِيَّةَ).

FREE · Useful verbs

Everyday action verbs

  • يَقْرَأُ — yaqraʾu — he reads
  • يَكْتُبُ — yaktubu — he writes
  • يَذْهَبُ — yadhhabu — he goes
  • يَفْهَمُ — yafhamu — he understands
  • يَسْمَعُ — yasmaʿu — he hears

Try changing each verb into a sentence using أَنَا (I). Example: أَنَا أَقْرَأُ — I read.

Words used in Islamic learning

  • صَلَاةٌ — ṣalātun — prayer
  • قُرْآنٌ — qurʾānun — Qur’an
  • عِلْمٌ — ʿilmun — knowledge
  • دُعَاءٌ — duʿāʾun — supplication

These are vocabulary notes for language study, not religious rulings or tafsīr.
