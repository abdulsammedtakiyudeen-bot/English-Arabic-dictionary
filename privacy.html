Privacy information

Information stored on your device

This version does not require an account. Saved words, learned-word selections, quiz correct-answer counts, theme preference, and saved online vocabulary are stored in your browser's local storage on your device. This data is not synced to a server by this app.

Optional online translation

If you choose “Try online translation,” the text you search for is sent to the third-party MyMemory translation service. The service may be unavailable, rate-limited, or inaccurate. Review that provider's current privacy terms before using this feature. Do not rely on machine translation for Qur'anic interpretation, religious rulings, or other high-stakes decisions.

Offline operation

The app uses a service worker to cache core files for offline use. Browser storage and cache can be cleared by your browser or device at any time.

Advertising and payments

This version contains only a visual advertisement placeholder. It does not include an active advertising SDK, analytics service, account system, subscription, or payment processing.

Contact and changes

This is a starter privacy notice and not legal advice. Add a verified contact method and revise this page if third-party services, advertising, analytics, accounts, or payments are introduced.
