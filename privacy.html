Privacy information

This starter version stores favourites, learned-word selections, quiz-answer counts, appearance settings, and saved online vocabulary in your browser's local storage on this device. It does not require an account.

If you choose online translation, the word you enter is sent to the third-party MyMemory translation service. Its availability, terms, and privacy practices may change; review the provider's current policies before public release.

The interface includes a reserved advertisement area, but no advertising SDK or advertising code is active in this starter version. If advertising, analytics, accounts, or payment services are added later, update this notice and comply with the relevant platform policies.

This is an initial project privacy notice, not legal advice. Review and adapt it before publishing publicly or submitting to an app store.
