Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

This policy explains how the current version of Islamic Arabic Learning Dictionary handles information. If the site's features or third-party services change, this policy must be updated to match the live website.

Information stored on your device

The app does not require an account. It may store saved words, learned-word selections, quiz progress, theme preferences, and related settings in your browser's local storage on your device. This app does not currently sync that data to its own server. You can clear local storage through your browser settings, though this may erase saved progress.

Optional online translation

If you choose the optional online translation feature, the search text may be sent to the third-party MyMemory translation service. That service operates under its own privacy practices and availability limits. Avoid entering personal or confidential information into translation tools, and check the provider's current terms and privacy information before use.

Cookies, analytics, and advertising

The current project files include a visual advertisement placeholder. A placeholder is not an active ad. This policy does not claim that Google AdSense, analytics, or advertising cookies are active. If advertising, analytics, or other tracking services are added later, this policy must be revised to describe the actual services and data practices. Any consent or privacy controls required for the site's visitors and applicable laws must also be implemented before those services are used.

Offline storage

The website may use a service worker to cache site files for offline access. Your browser manages this cache, and you can clear it using browser settings.

Third-party links

The site may link to external services, including its source-code issue tracker and an optional translation provider. We do not control their content or privacy practices. Review the relevant third party's policies before using those services.

Children and personal information

Do not submit sensitive personal information through public issue trackers or third-party services. If you have a privacy concern, use the contact information on our Contact page.

Changes to this policy

This page may be updated when the website changes. The date at the top should be revised when a meaningful update is published.

Contact

For questions about this policy, visit Contact Us.

This page is a practical website notice, not legal advice. The site owner should verify it against the actual live features and services before applying for advertising.
