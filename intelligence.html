A SIMPLE STUDY ROUTINE

Make a little progress every day.

Try a short vocabulary review, complete one lesson, then test yourself. Return to difficult words instead of rushing through new material.

  1. Review familiar words.
  2. Learn one new concept.
  3. Practise listening or reading.
  4. Check what you remember.
